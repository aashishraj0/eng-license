Computer Engineering Syllabus (ACiE)

1. Concept of Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering (AExE01)

1.1 Basic concept:

  • Ohm´s law
  • electric voltage current
  • power and energy
  • conducting and insulating materials
  • Series and parallel electric circuits
  • start-delta and delta-star conversion
  • Kirchhoff’s law
  • linear and non-linear circuit
  • bilateral and unilateral circuits
  • active and passive circuits

  • Concept of superposition theorem
  • Thevenin's theorem
  • Norton's theorem
  • maximum power transfer theorem
  • R-L, R-C, R-L-C circuits
  • resonance in AC series and parallel circuit
  • active and reactive power

  • Principle of generation of alternating voltages and currents and their equations and waveforms
  • average, peak and rms values
  • Three phase system

  • Semiconductor diode and its characteristics
  • BJT configuration and biasing

  • Basic principles of oscillator
  • RC, LC and crystal oscillators circuits
  • Waveform generators

  • Classification of output stages
  • Class A output stage
  • Class B output stage
  • Class AB output stage
  • biasing the Class AB stage
  • power BJTs
  • transformer-coupled push-pull stages
  • tuned amplifiers
  • op-amps
